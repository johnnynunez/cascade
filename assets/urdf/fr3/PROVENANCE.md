# Provenance

- Upstream: `frankarobotics/franka_description` (formerly frankaemika)
- Commit: `7aeeddc449edf8d62b594f9e36a81da53e7796f9` (main HEAD, 2026-09-09)
- License: Apache-2.0 (repository LICENSE)
- `fr3_full.urdf` is the xacro expansion of `robots/fr3/fr3.urdf.xacro` with
  `robot_type:=fr3 hand:=true ee_id:=franka_hand include_self_collision_geometry:=false with_sc:=false`,
  produced with the `xacro` PyPI package outside ROS (a two-function
  `ament_index_python` shim resolved `$(find franka_description)`).
  Kinematics come from `robots/fr3/kinematics.yaml`, joint limits from
  `robots/fr3/joint_limits.yaml`, inertials from `robots/fr3/inertials.yaml`
  and `end_effectors/franka_hand/inertials.yaml` -- all upstream data.
- `fr3_arm.urdf` is DERIVED, not upstream: generated by
  `python scripts/extract_arm_urdf.py fr3_full.urdf --root fr3_link0 \
      --tip fr3_hand --name fr3_arm --tcp-offset 0,0,0.1034`
  The TCP frame equals franka_description's `fr3_hand_tcp` (0.1034 m along
  the hand z axis, i.e. the fingertip centre with the stock fingers). The
  fixed `fr3_link8 -> fr3_hand` joint carries the -45 deg hand rotation.
- `fr3_hand_tcp` in the arm URDF has the SAME pose as
  `assets/mjcf/fr3/scene_hand.xml`'s attached hand body + 0.1034 m (MuJoCo
  side composed by `scripts/fetch_robot_assets.py fr3`, from
  google-deepmind/mujoco_menagerie `franka_fr3/` + `franka_emika_panda/hand.xml`
  at `da76818e269b82289eba39808e2fb91d679d6994`); tests/test_kinematics_fr3.py
  pins the two models to agree to < 0.1 mm over 50 random q.
- Real-robot transport: `franka_ros2` (humble branch, `fr3_arm_controller`
  joint_trajectory_controller + `franka_gripper` GripperCommand action),
  see configs/arms/fr3.yaml.
