01 / LOOK

Choose a view

Worktop shows objects and the green delivery square. Side shows lifts and releases. Kitchen shows the whole room.

02 / ASK

Try a request

Type “What can you see on the table?” or “Please put the orange in the open box.” in OpenClaw: the chat box on this page, or the OpenClaw chat at the booth.

03 / CHECK

Watch the result

Look for the object lifting, leaving the gripper and settling in the destination. Read the result with staff; reset after the task finishes.

This page shows cameras only. The demo uses prepared objects in simulation. Grasps can miss, and camera previews update only a few times per second.
