A short talk track

Introduce the demo in a few words.

Say: “This is PAAI — Physical Agentic AI. Ask about the kitchen or give the robot a task. OpenClaw uses CASCADE tools to act in NVIDIA Isaac Sim, and we check what actually happened.”

Show: point to the OpenClaw message box and the three live camera views. Explain that Worktop shows objects and destinations, while Side shows whether an object lifts.

Invite: “First ask it to look around. Then ask it to put the green cube in the green square. We will watch the lift and check the result.”

During the pick: point out the active tool in OpenClaw, the cube leaving the worktop, and the gripper opening over the green square. A pick can take about one to two minutes. Wait for the tool result before sending another order.

Visitor examples

Start with an everyday request.

  • “What can you see on the worktop?”
  • “Where is the green delivery zone?”
  • “Put the orange in the green square.”
  • “Move the tomato can into the green square.”
  • “Reset the scene.”

Visitors can use their own words. Help them name one visible object and one destination at a time. Use the steps below if they need more guidance.

Explain the result plainly.

“The gripper missed, so this attempt did not move the object. We can reset and try another request.” Only call a grasp successful when the camera and physics result support it.

If an object is missing or the request is unclear, ask the visitor to choose a visible object or clarify the destination. Keep the failed result, let the current tool finish, and use the reset steps below.

This is a simulation with a small set of objects and prepared robot skills. The lemon and unusual arrangements can be unreliable. Camera previews update only a few times per second; they are useful for watching the result, not timing a fast movement.

Follow the five steps in order

Walk the visitor through one complete task.

One task. Three camera views. Clear feedback.

  1. 01

    Open your conversation

    Click Open OpenClaw above. Use the new OpenClaw tab for the visitor. Keep the live camera page beside that chat; this guide is your presenter reference.

    The monitor beneath the button shows Waiting, Active, and Done as each real check completes.

    Success looks like: a connected chat with a message box you can type in.

    If it stops: read the unfinished check, then click Retry connection.

    Refresh guide status →
  2. 02

    Find the green cube

    Use the camera buttons above, or click See all three cameras. Choose Worktop to see the cube and the arm's working area.

    Kitchen shows the whole scene. Side makes the arm's lift easier to follow.

    Success looks like: a current image with a Live label.

    Live previews can update only a few times per second. Look for the Live label and advancing views; a low frame rate alone does not mean the kitchen is still starting.

    If a camera disconnects: keep the window open while it reconnects. Use this guide's camera buttons to check each current view before moving.

    Keep cameras beside the chat

    The optional Physical Agentic AI · OpenClaw Demo browser extension opens the views in a side panel. Click Connect cameras in the panel and allow the connection if asked.

    You can also keep the camera page and the chat in two browser windows.

    Ask the booth staff for help installing the optional camera extension.

  3. 03

    Observe before moving

    Click Copy order and paste this line into OpenClaw's message box. Use the send arrow to submit the order. Wait for the full reply. Check the object colors and positions against Worktop before moving.

    This order gives OpenClaw a fresh image to inspect, along with camera freshness, configured destinations and robot status. A listed destination does not prove that the area is visible or empty; compare the answer with Worktop.

    Call get_observation once for a fresh view. Describe only the objects, colors and shapes visible in the returned image. Say when something is unclear; do not treat configured destinations as proof that an area is visible or empty. Use at most three short sentences. Do not move anything or call another tool.

    For a separate Worktop image, use this optional snapshot order. Compare its reply with the live cameras before moving.

    Call camera_snapshot exactly once with camera="isaac". Inspect only fully visible foreground tabletop objects; ignore the plate and anything cut off by the image edge. Reply in three short sentences: first describe their colors and shapes; second describe the visible interior of the wooden box; third locate the green square relative to the box. Keep every claim limited to visible features. Do not move anything or call another tool.

    Camera Snapshot sends a still image to OpenClaw for inspection. This version may show Omitted from history instead of a preview; compare its reply with the live Worktop view beside the chat. The still image does not update as the robot moves.

    Success looks like: OpenClaw uses Get Observation or Camera Snapshot and describes what is visible in that image, stating uncertainty where needed. The arm stays still.

    If the tool reports an old or unavailable image: wait for the live cameras to recover, then send the observation order once before requesting any movement.

    If the description does not match Worktop: keep the discrepancy visible and ask for a fresh observation. For a measured position, ask OpenClaw to locate the named object. Keep the green cube distinct from the green delivery square.

  4. 04

    Make one simple move

    Keep Worktop visible. Find the thick green square painted on the counter; the cube should fit fully inside its border. Copy and send the next order in the same conversation. Let the arm finish before sending another command.

    Pick up the green cube and put it in the green square. Then stop.

    Success looks like: the arm reaches for the green cube, carries it, and releases it fully inside the green square on the counter.

    If it misses or stops: keep the reply and ask for the physics check below. Do not repeat the movement yet.

  5. 05

    Ask what actually happened

    After the movement finishes, copy and send this final order. A camera image helps you watch; physics evidence checks the result.

    Check the green cube's position using physics. Did the grasp succeed, and did the cube land inside the green square?

    Success looks like: a clear answer about the cube's position, the grasp, and the placement, including any failure.

    If the result is uncertain: ask which check is missing. Keep the result and ask the booth operator to review it before sending another movement.

Between visitors

Reset for the next person.

Wait for the previous order to finish. Send the reset order once in the same OpenClaw conversation.

Call reset_scene NOW to perform a NEW physical reset. You must make a fresh tool call before answering; do not reuse an earlier reset result. Call reset_scene exactly once and no other tools. After the tool returns, reply briefly in English, then stop.

Wait for the reset reply and for all three camera views to return. This optional status readback reports cached camera counters; compare the current views to check the reset.

Call world_state exactly once. Report the frame_id values for isaac, isaac_side, and kitchen from this tool result as cached client delivery counters. These counters alone do not verify live cameras or a completed reset. Do not move or reset anything. Do not use another tool. Reply briefly in English.

Look for: a successful reset reply, an empty gripper at home, and the green and pink cubes, can, lemon, and loose orange back at their original positions in Worktop.

Let the reset finish before handing over. A new conversation alone does not return the objects to their starting positions.

A second destination

The open box

Reset the scene first using the steps above. Find the shallow wooden box beside the green square in Worktop. Its opening faces up.

Pick up the green cube and put it inside the open box. Then stop.

Success looks like: the gripper releases the cube onto the box floor, with space between the cube and all four walls. The cube is taller than the shallow box, so its top stays visible above the rim.

Watch the release in Worktop and Side. Wait for the full reply, then reset before the next visitor.

The pink cube with the 3×3 pattern also fits in the box. After a reset, try this order:

Pick up the pink cube and put it inside the open box. Then stop.

Look for the pink cube resting on the box floor with space around all four sides. Wait for the full reply and reset before another order.

Tomato can

After a reset, find the red-and-cream can with tomato pictures in Worktop. Its metal lid is closed. Keep the green square empty and wait for the previous task to finish.

Pick up the tomato can and put it in the green square. Then stop.

Success looks like: both jaws hold the can clear of the table, then open to leave it standing fully inside the green border. Watch the lift in Side and the final position in Worktop.

Wait for the full reply, then reset before the next visitor.

Lemon

After a reset, find the yellow oval fruit in Worktop. It should be the only yellow grasp target within reach. Keep the green square empty.

Pick up the lemon and put it in the green square. Then stop.

Success looks like: both jaws lift the lemon clear of the table, then release it fully inside the green border. Watch the lift in Side and the settled fruit in Worktop.

Wait for the full reply. If the pick fails, read the failure and stop; do not substitute another object. Reset before the next visitor.

Loose orange

After a reset, find the single loose orange close to the arm in Worktop. The oranges on the plate are outside the grasp area. Keep the green square empty.

Pick up the orange and put it in the green square. Then stop.

Success looks like: both jaws lift the loose orange clear of the table, then release it fully inside the green border. Watch the lift in Side and the settled fruit in Worktop.

Wait for the full reply. If the pick fails, read the failure and stop; do not substitute another orange. Reset before the next visitor.

If something is stuck

The message box says “Offline”. Click Open OpenClaw above and confirm the message box is connected before submitting an order.

The camera says “Cameras offline”. Wait for the Live label before judging a movement.

The live camera window says “Camera offline”. Let any reset finish, then use this guide's camera buttons to check Kitchen, Worktop and Side. Wait for a current scene image before sending the next movement. The separate camera page reports connections; its label does not check whether each image is advancing.

The arm is still busy. Wait for the assistant's reply before sending another movement.
